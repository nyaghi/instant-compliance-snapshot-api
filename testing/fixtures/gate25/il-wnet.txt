Use this online tool to search for charitable organizations that are compliant with the registration and reporting requirements of the Charitable Trust Act and Solicitation for Charity Act. The search will provide financial information about registered charities, such as assets and income, and provide access to downloadable copies of charitable organizations’ annual financial reports.  Please note that charitable organizations prepare their own reports, and the Attorney General’s office cannot guarantee the accuracy, quality or validity of the information.

If a charity is not currently registered with the Attorney General’s office, it will not be listed in the search results. However, the Attorney General’s office maintains a separate record of every charity ever registered. If you cannot find a charity in the search, feel free to call 312-814-2595 (7-1-1 Relay System) to find out if the charity was ever registered.
