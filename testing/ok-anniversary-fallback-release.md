# Oklahoma calculated registration expiration — .9 staging

User explicitly authorized a filing-anniversary fallback after reviewing its limits. The master continues to prefer a fresh certificate and an eligible verified certificate cached for less than 24 hours. Only certificate transport failure can trigger the new calculation. The live matched registry must have a fully parsed registration or renewal row. Renewal Registration - EZ is also a qualifying filing type, observed in Reading Is Fundamental's live history.

The calculated expiration is the same month/day in the following calendar year. This represents the user-selected registration-expiration interpretation, not a determination of the separate Form 990-based statutory filing deadline. Public comments begin with the calculated date and disclose that the certificate was unavailable and the date is not certificate-confirmed. That disclosure survives PDF evidence excerpt shortening.

Invalid retrieved certificates, missing certificate actions, uncertain or future filing dates, duplicate documents on the same latest filing date, unparseable qualifying history, February 29, and adverse/pending/rejected registry evidence cannot use the fallback. Amendments and unrelated filings do not reset the anniversary. No organization-specific names, EINs, dates or runtime sidecars were added. No shared matching, batch or report-template code changed.
