Arizona (AZ)—State Guide

Arizona charitable solicitation registration requirements

Arizona generally does not require a standard state charitable solicitation registration filing from charities before fundraising. That does not make Arizona a no-rules state: solicitation conduct remains regulated, special rules apply to solicitations in the name of American veterans, and a nonprofit fundraising nationally may still have registration duties elsewhere.

Use this Arizona charitable solicitation requirements overview to separate the general registration rule from the exceptions, fundraising safeguards, and multi-state work that may still apply.

Does Arizona require charitable solicitation registration?

For most charities, Arizona does not have the recurring charitable solicitation registration and renewal system used by many other states. Arizona repealed its general charity-registration statutes in 2013. A narrow exception remains for organizations soliciting money or support in the name of American veterans, which must file a registration statement with the Arizona Secretary of State.

Arizona fundraising compliance checklist

  • Confirm that the organization is not soliciting in the name of American veterans or within another special regulatory category.
  • Use accurate charity names, affiliations, endorsements, and statements in every appeal; Arizona law prohibits material solicitation misrepresentations.
  • Review contracts and responsibilities when a paid or contracted fundraiser participates in the campaign.
  • Map every state reached by the campaign, because Arizona's general rule does not remove registration obligations in other jurisdictions.
  • Keep campaign approvals, fundraiser contracts, solicitation copy, donation records, and state filing evidence in a reviewable archive.

What online fundraising changes

A Donate button, email campaign, social post, or crowdfunding page can reach donors outside Arizona. The practical question is therefore not only whether Arizona requires a filing, but which other states the organization is actively soliciting in and whether registrations, renewals, exemptions, or recovery filings are due there.

FAQ

Does Arizona require charitable solicitation registration?

Generally, Arizona does not require the standard charity registration filing used in many states. Organizations soliciting in the name of American veterans are a specific exception, and other laws still regulate solicitation conduct.

Do we need to register in Arizona if we have a Donate button but don’t intentionally target Arizona donors?

A Donate button can reach donors outside Arizona. Even when Arizona does not require a general filing, the organization should review whether its campaign creates registration obligations in other states.

What Arizona fundraising rules still matter?

Charities should avoid false or misleading solicitation claims, use names and endorsements accurately, review paid-fundraiser arrangements, and keep evidence supporting campaign representations.

How should a nonprofit handle multi-state online fundraising?

Map where the organization actively solicits, check each state's registration and exemption rules, then maintain a calendar and filing archive. CharityClarity Aurora can provide a quick snapshot, while a deeper review can cover the broader fundraising footprint.
