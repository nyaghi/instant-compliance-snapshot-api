Texas category-specific charitable solicitation renewal guide

Texas charity registration renewal

Because Texas has no general charitable solicitation registration for most charities, it also has no universal charity renewal. Renewal is category-specific. Covered law-enforcement telephone, public-safety, and veterans solicitation records use different agencies, forms, fees, bonds, and reports. The right workflow starts by preserving the original category and filing number.

Texas at a glance

  • General rule: There is no universal Texas charity-solicitation renewal.
  • LETSA: Covered law-enforcement telephone records use the OAG renewal statement.
  • Public safety: Form 3201 is used for organization or promoter registration and renewal.
  • Veterans: The Form 3500 series includes registration, bond, annual, and solicitor reports.
Scope note: This page covers renewal of Texas special solicitation registrations identified by the Attorney General and Secretary of State. General nonprofit entity reports, federal filings, private-foundation Form 990-PF delivery, raffles, bingo, local matters, and other jurisdictions remain separate. This guide is informational and not legal or tax advice.

Identify the original Texas registration category

Start with the last accepted filing, agency, registration number, legal and assumed names, participant type, bond, expiration, reporting history, and solicitation method. Do not put every charity on an annual renewal calendar. The Attorney General says most charities do not register generally, while specific law-enforcement telephone, public-safety, and veterans categories have their own continuing filings.

Re-screen the campaign facts each cycle. A new telephone solicitor, independent promoter, public-safety publication, veterans-related name, beneficiary representation, or contract can add a participant or change the category. Save the analysis alongside the renewal so the next filer understands why the form exists.

Renew a covered LETSA organization with the OAG

The OAG’s LETSA form includes both Initial Registration and Renewal Registration. It asks for the registrant, chapters and affiliates, registered agent, officers and directors, solicitors, tax status, and financial information. Reconcile the renewal to the current call contracts, scripts, caller list, federal return, and prior registry record before an officer certifies it.

The Attorney General’s program page lists a $50 organization filing fee and a separate $50,000 commercial telephone solicitor bond filed with the Secretary of State when applicable. Preserve the bond status and do not assume the organization renewal extends or replaces a solicitor’s security. Save the submitted form, fee, delivery, and updated OAG registry result.

Use the Form 3200 series for public-safety renewals

Form 3201 is the multi-purpose Public Safety Organization, Independent Promoter, or Public Safety Publication Registration/Renewal Statement. It collects the registrant, each solicitor, each beneficiary organization or fund, use of contributions, tax status, and other required facts. The Secretary of State FAQ lists the $250 filing fee for that participant type.

A solicitor uses the separate Form 3203 renewal route and applicable bond. Reconcile organization, promoter, publication, and solicitor records so every active participant is covered. If a contract ended, document the effective date and any final-report obligation instead of silently removing the party from the current form.

Maintain veterans registrations, bonds, and reports

The Secretary of State’s Form 3500 series routes veterans organization registration or renewal, the organization bond, the organization annual report, solicitor registration or renewal, solicitor bond, and solicitor quarterly reports. Build a requirements table by participant because the organization and the solicitor have different documents and reporting cadences.

Reconcile veterans-related names, chapters, officers, solicitors, contribution use, bond terms, and expenditure reporting. Do not assume that submitting the annual report renews the registration or that renewing the organization covers the solicitor. Use the current form labels and agency instructions tied to the existing record.

Verify each accepted record and calendar its own date

Save the category analysis, completed form, bond, financial or activity report, fee, delivery, and agency confirmation. Confirm the OAG registry or Secretary of State filing record when available. A mailing receipt is evidence of delivery, not state acceptance, and registration never implies endorsement.

Calendar the expiration, renewal preparation, bond continuation, annual or quarterly report, contract review, and status recheck separately for each participant. For an ordinary charity outside the special categories, preserve the no-general-registration analysis and monitor campaign changes rather than manufacturing a renewal filing.

Texas renewal FAQ

Does every Texas charity renew a solicitation registration?

No. Most charities do not have a general Texas registration. Renewal applies only when a specific regulated category covers the organization or solicitor.

How does a LETSA organization renew?

Use the OAG form marked Renewal Registration and update the organization, affiliates, leaders, solicitors, tax status, and financial information.

What form renews a Texas public-safety organization?

Form 3201 is the multi-purpose registration and renewal statement for a public-safety organization, promoter, or publication.

Does the public-safety organization renewal cover its solicitor?

No. The solicitor uses a separate registration or renewal statement and applicable bond.

What ongoing veterans forms may apply?

The Form 3500 series separates organization registration, bond, annual report, solicitor registration, solicitor bond, and quarterly reports.

When is a Texas special-category renewal complete?

Use the accepted agency record, maintained bond, and required reports. A mailing receipt or fee payment alone is not completion.
