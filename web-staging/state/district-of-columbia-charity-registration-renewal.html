District of Columbia renewal guide

DC charity registration renewal

A practical guide to renewing a District of Columbia charitable solicitation license: confirm the license term and expiration, refresh the prerequisite records, use the current DLCP portal route, and verify the renewed public record.

DC renewal at a glance

  • Agency: DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection (DLCP).
  • License activity: Charitable Solicitation under Charitable Services Business.
  • Published terms and fees: 2-year license for $99 or 4-year license for $198.
  • Public check: use SCOUT to confirm the license record.

Identify the license that actually needs renewal

A DC charitable solicitation license is not the same record as the nonprofit's corporate registration, biennial report, trade name, federal tax exemption, or DC tax standing. DLCP lists corporate registration and a Clean Hands/tax record among the charitable-services requirements, which means an issue in a prerequisite record can interrupt the license workflow even when the prior solicitation license exists.

Build the renewal readiness packet

Start from the personalized checklist in the current licensing portal because DLCP may request additional records. The public Charitable Services page currently lists the following items for the business activity:

  • Certificate of Occupancy or Home Occupation Permit information.
  • The Charitable Services Business Worksheet.
  • Corporate registration, when applicable.
  • The IRS determination letter.
  • Certificate of Exemption/FR-164 exemption information.
  • Tax registration and a Clean Hands certificate.

For the Charitable Solicitation category, the worksheet contains a certified resolution authorizing the named person to submit the application and certifying the submitted information. Route that authorization early so the filing does not stall at the signature or notary step.

Calendar the term, fee, and verification checkpoints

60–90 days before expiration

Search SCOUT, save the existing license, confirm the selected 2- or 4-year term, and inventory corporate, tax, occupancy, exemption, and authorization records.

At submission

Use the current DLCP portal, preserve the completed checklist and worksheet, pay the live fee, and save the portal receipt.

After processing

Recheck SCOUT, save the renewed license and expiration date, and calendar the next term before closing the task.

Current published fee: DLCP's Charitable Services Business page lists $99 for a 2-year Charitable Solicitation license and $198 for a 4-year license. Confirm the live fee and term in the portal at filing because the agency can update licensing procedures.

Renew through the current account path

  1. Open the current license portal from the official DLCP Charitable Services page and sign in through Access DC.
  2. Locate the existing charitable solicitation license rather than beginning an unrelated corporate filing.
  3. Confirm the requested term, organization details, business activity, and prerequisite records against the saved evidence packet.
  4. Upload or complete the records shown on the personalized checklist and resolve any Clean Hands, corporate, or authorization problem before expiration.
  5. Save the payment and submission confirmation, monitor notices, then use SCOUT to verify the renewed public license record.

If the portal does not show the expected license during the BOSS transition, avoid creating a duplicate record. Use DLCP's published licensing contact route and provide the existing license number, organization name, and expiration date.

DC renewal FAQ

How long is a DC charitable solicitation license term?

DLCP currently lists a 2-year Charitable Solicitation license for $99 and a 4-year license for $198. Confirm the term and expiration on the organization's actual license and in the live portal.

Is a DC charitable solicitation renewal the same as a corporate biennial report?

No. The charitable solicitation license, corporate registration, biennial report, and tax/Clean Hands records are separate. A problem in a prerequisite record can still interrupt the license renewal.

What records should a nonprofit prepare for DC renewal?

The public DLCP checklist currently includes the Charitable Services worksheet, corporate registration when applicable, IRS determination letter, exemption and tax/Clean Hands records, and occupancy information. The personalized portal checklist controls the actual filing.

Where does a nonprofit renew a DC charitable solicitation license?

Enter through the current portal link on DLCP's Charitable Services Business page and sign in through Access DC. This is especially important during the August 2026 BOSS system transition.

How can a nonprofit verify a DC renewal?

Keep the portal and payment confirmation, then use SCOUT to confirm the renewed license status and expiration date. Save the renewed public record before closing the compliance task.
