Pennsylvania charity registration requirements: operational summary

Operational summary for BCO filing, BCO-10 renewal timing, exemptions, documents, audit thresholds, late fees, and risk points.

Requirement Pennsylvania (PA)
Filing Authority Pennsylvania Department of State → Bureau of Charitable Organizations (BCO)
Filing Method Online via BCO portal for renewals/updates (some filings may be mailed as permitted)
Filing Calendar & Triggers Annual renewal for registered charities (fiscal-year based)
Filing Due Date 15th day of the 11th month after fiscal year end (BCO-10)
Renewal Detail File BCO-10 + attachments; tiered fee; update officers/address and solicitation methods
Extensions Calendar Extension generally aligned to IRS extension (submit extension/BCO guidance as applicable)
Exemptions Religious organizations; educational institutions; certain small/local fundraising; and other statutory exemptions
Good Standing Required for Charity Filing? Yes
Solicitation while Curing Deficiencies? No
Enforcement Posture High
Recovery Method File missing BCO-10 renewals from oldest to newest with the required fees; resolve deficiency letters; reinstate if revoked.
Required Documents BCO-10; IRS Form 990 series; financial statements; professional fundraiser contracts if applicable
Financial Audit/Review Thresholds Audit required above threshold contributions; CPA review may be required in mid-tier (per PA instructions)
Schedule B Reaction Rule (Donor Names) Keep (PA typically requires complete IRS return; donor info not publicly disclosed by BCO)
Signature Requirements Authorized officer signature; electronic accepted for online filing
High-Risk Traps / Enforcement Notes
Due date is later than IRS; ensure correct fee tier; missing audited financials where required blocks renewal
High Late fee alert: monthly penalties that accumulate.
