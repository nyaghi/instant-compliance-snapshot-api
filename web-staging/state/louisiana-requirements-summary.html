Requirement Louisiana (LA)
Filing Authority Louisiana Department of Justice (Attorney General) â†’ Public Protection Division
Filing Method
  • Submit URS + attachments; pay fee online or by mail

Filing Calendar & Triggers
  • Charities that solicit in LA using a professional solicitor must register annually
  • Registration must be filed at least 10 days prior to soliciting
Filing Due Date At least 10 days before solicitation; renew annually thereafter.
Renewal Detail
  • Renew annually using URS + renewal checklist items
