Massachusetts Form PC annual filing guide

Massachusetts charity registration renewal

Massachusetts public charities file Form PC and the required attachments through the Charity Portal. A reliable renewal process calculates the four-and-one-half-month deadline, verifies whether the compliance-dependent extension applies, selects the correct accountant’s report, routes portal signatures and payment, and preserves the submitted public record.

Massachusetts at a glance

  • Regulator: Massachusetts Attorney General, Non-Profit Organizations/Public Charities Division.
  • Annual report: Form PC and the attachments required by the organization’s filing profile.
  • General deadline: Four and one-half months after fiscal year end.
  • Filing channel: Massachusetts Charity Portal; paper annual filings are no longer accepted.
Scope note: This page covers the Massachusetts Attorney General annual filing for public charities. Massachusetts corporate annual reports, federal tax returns, tax exemption, local permits, gaming filings, and paid-fundraiser obligations are separate. Boundary cases and portal prompts should be confirmed with the Division. This guide is informational and not legal or tax advice.

Calculate the filing date and test the extension condition

The Massachusetts Attorney General says an annual Form PC filing is due four and one-half months after fiscal year end. A December 31 year generally points to May 15, while a June 30 year generally points to November 15. Record the exact fiscal period and the date displayed in the Charity Portal; do not substitute the federal return deadline or the corporation’s annual report date.

Massachusetts describes an automatic six-month extension for a registered charity that is in compliance with its annual reporting requirements. No written request is needed for an eligible charity. That condition matters: an organization with missing prior reports should not assume that the extension protects the current filing. Check the portal record early and resolve prior-year gaps before relying on the extended date.

Build one portal-ready Form PC evidence packet

Confirm the AG Number, portal users, fiscal period, federal-return status, solicitation activity, related organizations, fundraising relationships, gross support and revenue, accountant’s report requirement, signers, and fee band before starting. Assemble final PDFs with stable names and reconcile Form PC figures to the federal return and financial statements before anyone signs.

The official Form PC instructions identify solicitation schedules, related-organization disclosures, and the applicable federal return or probate account among the possible attachments. The old paper instructions are useful for document planning, but the Charity Portal is now the required filing channel and its current questions control. If a federal filing profile, annual exemption, or special entity type does not fit the ordinary workflow, follow the live portal prompts and Division guidance rather than forcing an attachment assumption.

Apply the current CPA review and audit thresholds carefully

Massachusetts changed the statutory thresholds effective November 20, 2024. The Charity Portal notice states that a CPA review report threshold is now more than $500,000 and the audit threshold is more than $1,000,000 in gross support and revenue. The Attorney General FAQ describes the middle band as at least $500,000 but less than $1,000,000 and the audit band as $1,000,000 or more. Because the public wording is not perfectly uniform at the exact boundaries, confirm the portal result when the organization is at $500,000 or $1,000,000.

Below the review threshold, Form PC and all other required attachments still apply. In the middle band, plan for an independent CPA review or audit; above the audit threshold, plan for audited financial statements. Engage the accountant early enough to finish the report, reconcile it to the federal return and Form PC, and route the final version rather than a draft.

Calculate the statutory fee from gross support and revenue

The current published schedule is $35 when gross support and revenue is not more than $100,000; $70 when it is more than $100,000 through $250,000; $125 when it is more than $250,000 through $500,000; $250 when it is more than $500,000 through $1 million; $500 when it is more than $1 million through $10 million; $1,000 when it is more than $10 million through $100 million; and $2,000 when it is more than $100 million.

Preserve the calculation and the amount displayed by the portal. Treat payment as part of filing completion, not as a later accounting task. Identify the authorized payment owner and method before signatures are routed so the filing does not stall after approval.

Track signatures, payment, submission, and public posting separately

Create individual portal access for the preparer, required signers, and payment owner as appropriate. Monitor the filing through the current portal handoffs: data entry and uploads, signature approval, payment availability, payment confirmation, official submission, agency correspondence, and eventual public posting. An approved signature or a payment prompt is not final completion.

Save the submitted Form PC, every attachment, the CPA report when required, signature evidence, payment receipt, portal confirmation, and later public filing result. If the Division asks for more information, keep the filing open until the response is accepted. Massachusetts renewal FAQ

When is Massachusetts Form PC due?

The Attorney General says the annual filing is due four and one-half months after the charity’s fiscal year ends.

Is the Massachusetts Form PC extension automatic?

A registered charity that is in compliance with annual reporting requirements receives an automatic six-month extension. Verify prior-year compliance before relying on it.

Can Massachusetts Form PC be filed on paper?

No. The Attorney General says charity registrations and annual filings must be submitted through the Charity Portal.

When is a Massachusetts CPA review or audit required?

Current state guidance uses the $500,000 and $1,000,000 gross-support-and-revenue boundaries. Because wording differs at the exact endpoints, confirm the live portal result for a boundary-year filer.

How much is the Massachusetts annual charity filing fee?

The statutory schedule ranges from $35 through $2,000 based on gross support and revenue. Preserve the calculation and the amount displayed in the portal.

Does a Massachusetts portal signature mean Form PC is filed?

Not necessarily. Track the filing through all required signatures, payment, official submission, any agency response, and the later public filing record.
