Kansas expired charitable registration recovery guide

Kansas expired charity registration recovery

Kansas does not publish a separate late-registration or reinstatement form for an expired charitable solicitation certificate. Its current instructions use the regular registration statement for initial and renewal filings, require the complete $25 packet, and state that Kansas does not allow late registrations even though it does not add a separate late fee.

Kansas at a glance

  • Regulator: Kansas Attorney General, Charitable Organization Registration Unit.
  • Expiration: Last day of the sixth month after the charity’s fiscal year ends.
  • Late-extension rule: Kansas says an IRS extension does not permit late state registration.
  • Filing amount: $25 registration fee; current instructions say there is no additional late fee.
Scope note: This page covers the Kansas Attorney General charitable organization registration certificate. Kansas corporate records, federal tax exemption, professional fundraiser licensing, local requirements, and other states are separate. Whether an exemption applies is a legal question the Kansas Attorney General says its staff cannot decide for the organization.

Confirm expiration from the fiscal year and state record

K.S.A. 17-1763 states that a Kansas charitable solicitation certificate expires on the last day of the sixth month following the month in which the organization’s fiscal year ends. The Attorney General’s current instructions repeat that rule. For a calendar-year organization, the practical deadline is June 30. Save the public directory result, AG Charitable ID, fiscal year, latest certificate, filing history, and any correspondence before rebuilding the packet.

Do not substitute the IRS deadline for the Kansas expiration date. The state instructions expressly say Kansas law does not allow late registrations even when the IRS extends a federal tax deadline. That means an expired certificate is not kept alive by Form 8868. Build the recovery timeline from the Kansas date, and keep corporate annual reports and federal filings in separate workstreams.

Use the current registration statement as the renewal cure form

The Kansas form itself has checkboxes for initial registration and renewal; the state does not publish a separate charity reinstatement form on its registration page. Complete the current Charitable Organization Registration Statement for Solicitations with the AG ID and EIN, legal and solicitation names, entity information, Kansas offices and affiliates, officers, directors, trustees, principal salaried employees, financial-record custodian, contribution responsibilities, solicitation purpose, professional fundraiser use, other-state authorization, enforcement history, and fundraising costs.

Reconcile every field to approved corporate, board, tax, and campaign records. An expired record can be accompanied by staff turnover, an outdated mailing address, or a changed fundraiser, so copying last year’s form can reproduce the cause of the lapse. Use attachments where the form permits them and label each list to the matching question and fiscal period.

Assemble the complete financial and signature packet

The current Kansas instructions list four acceptance components: the $25 fee, completed registration statement, IRS Form 990 excluding Schedule B or the Kansas AG Financial Statement, and an audited financial statement when applicable. K.S.A. 17-1763 sets the audit threshold at contributions over $500,000 during the most recently completed fiscal year. An organization exempt from a federal return uses the AG financial form required by the packet.

Two separate authorized officers must sign under penalty of perjury, and one must be the chief fiscal officer. Confirm both signatures and dates after the final attachments have been reconciled. The instructions state that the complete packet is required to be accepted for filing. A check, an unsigned form, or a federal return without the state statement does not independently restore the registration record.

Treat no late fee as different from permission to file late

Kansas says it does not charge an additional fee for late registration, but the same instruction immediately says the law does not allow late registrations. Read those statements together: the organization still pays the ordinary $25 registration fee, but it should not present the lapse as an authorized extension or grace period. Confirm any record-specific instruction with the Attorney General registration unit rather than inventing a reinstatement fee or backdated certificate.

K.S.A. 17-1763 generally prohibits a nonexempt charitable organization from soliciting funds in Kansas unless it filed the registration statement before solicitation. Document current donation pages, appeals, events, grants, and professional fundraiser activity while the certificate is expired and obtain appropriate advice on interim decisions. The state’s statement that staff cannot decide exemption questions should also remain clear in the internal workpaper.

Track the packet through certificate and public verification

The Kansas Attorney General says it processes complete packets and mails a certificate of registration after processing. Preserve the delivered packet, fee evidence, carrier record, correspondence, and issued certificate. Do not treat delivery, a cashed check, or a draft public-directory entry as final approval. Route any deficiency question to one responsible person and answer it from the reconciled packet.

After the certificate arrives, check the official Kansas charity directory for the exact legal name and confirm the AG ID, registration status, and period. Save the dated result, then calendar the last day of the sixth month after the next fiscal year end and begin preparation before the federal return becomes the bottleneck. Kansas recovery FAQ

When does a Kansas charity registration expire?

The certificate expires on the last day of the sixth month following the month in which the charity’s fiscal year ends.

Does an IRS extension extend Kansas charity registration?

No. The Kansas Attorney General instructions state that Kansas does not allow late registration even when the IRS extends the federal tax deadline.

Is there a separate Kansas charity reinstatement form?

The current state page uses the Charitable Organization Registration Statement for both initial and renewal filings and does not list a separate charitable organization reinstatement form.

What belongs in the Kansas expired-registration packet?

Include the completed statement, $25 fee, Form 990 without Schedule B or the AG Financial Statement, any required audited financial statement, and two authorized officer signatures including the chief fiscal officer.

Does Kansas charge a charity late fee?

The current instructions say Kansas does not add a late-registration fee, but they also state that late registrations are not allowed. The ordinary $25 registration fee still applies.

When is Kansas charity registration recovery complete?

Use the mailed Attorney General certificate and the updated official charity directory as the acceptance evidence, then calendar the next state expiration date.
