Pennsylvania renewal guide

Pennsylvania charity registration renewal

A practical BCO-10 renewal workflow: read the expiration date on the current certificate, align the filing to the right fiscal year, apply Pennsylvania's contribution-based fee and financial-statement rules, and preserve proof through approval.

BCO-10 renewal at a glance

  • Regulator: Pennsylvania Department of State, Bureau of Corporations and Charitable Organizations.
  • Renewal statement: BCO-10 with the applicable IRS return, financial statements, fee, and other required records.
  • Due-date rule: no later than the 15th day of the 11th month after fiscal year end.
  • Certificate control: the certificate's “Expiration Date & Automatic Extension” is the next renewal due date.

Use the certificate date, not an improvised extension calendar

Pennsylvania's BCO-10 instructions say renewal must be refiled no later than the 15th day of the 11th month after fiscal year end. The Bureau now automatically grants the maximum extension when registration is approved, so a separate extension request is not needed for a charitable-organization BCO-10. The approved certificate shows the expiration date with that automatic extension, and the Bureau identifies that date as the next renewal deadline.

Do not merge two Pennsylvania regimes: the Bureau states that the automatic BCO-10 extension does not apply to Institution of Purely Public Charity registrations. An organization that has an IPP filing should calendar that obligation separately.

At approval

Save the certificate, expiration/automatic-extension date, certificate number, fiscal year, and organization contact email.

Before filing

Tie BCO-10 to the immediate preceding fiscal year, the correct federal return, national contributions, and required financial statements.

After filing

Keep the submission, payment, uploaded documents, notices, and the later approval certificate as separate evidence points.

Choose the fee and financial statements from national contributions

Pennsylvania bases the BCO-10 fee and financial-statement level on gross annual contributions from all sources, not only contributions from Pennsylvania. Use the current instructions and the immediate preceding fiscal year; do not substitute total revenue without following the form's contribution calculation.

The instructions also list a $15 fee and no financial statements for specified Section 162.7(a) organizations. Confirm whether that classification actually applies before using it.

Assemble the BCO-10 renewal package

  • Use the fiscal year that matches the most recently filed Form 990, 990-EZ, 990-PF, 990-N, or group return.
  • Complete and sign the BCO-10, list the legal name and all names used to solicit, and keep the certificate number with the payment record.
  • Attach the applicable IRS return and the correct financial statements for the contribution range.
  • Review the professional fundraising and solicitor questions against actual contracts and campaign activity.
  • Update the contact person, email, principal address, and mailing address so reminders, certificates, and deficiency notices reach an active owner.
Late-fee rule: the current instructions state that a filing after the due date carries an additional $25 for each calendar month or part of a month late. The instructions say the fee is statutory and cannot be waived. Calculate the late period before issuing payment rather than waiting for a deficiency notice.

File online with a clean evidence trail

  1. Create or use the authorized Keystone Login account and link the charity to the user's dashboard.
  2. Select the BCO-10 renewal workflow and confirm the organization, certificate, and fiscal-year information before uploading documents.
  3. Use alphanumeric file names. The Bureau warns that special characters such as & + # @ can prevent the system from receiving documents.
  4. Submit the complete packet, pay the calculated fee, and save the confirmation and every uploaded file.
  5. Monitor for a deficiency or approval and save the new certificate. A portal receipt proves submission; the approved certificate proves the renewed registration period.

The Bureau also accepts registrations by USPS regular mail and says emailed filings are not permitted. Use the current agency page to confirm the available route before sending sensitive filing records.

Pennsylvania renewal FAQ

When is a Pennsylvania BCO-10 renewal due?

The current instructions say no later than the 15th day of the 11th month after fiscal year end. Use the “Expiration Date & Automatic Extension” on the approved certificate as the next renewal date.

Does a Pennsylvania charity need to request a BCO-10 extension?

No separate request is needed for the charitable-organization BCO-10 because the Bureau says the maximum extension is automatically granted at approval. That statement does not apply to the separate Institution of Purely Public Charity registration.

How is the Pennsylvania BCO-10 fee calculated?

The published fee tiers use gross annual contributions from all sources for the immediate preceding fiscal year. They are not limited to donations received from Pennsylvania.

Can a Pennsylvania charity renew online?

Yes. The Bureau provides a Keystone Login and online BCO-10 renewal workflow. Link the entity, use alphanumeric file names, preserve the uploaded packet and payment confirmation, and monitor through approval.

What happens when a Pennsylvania renewal is late?

The current BCO-10 instructions state that an additional $25 is due for each calendar month or part of a month after the filing deadline.
