DC charitable solicitation registration requirements

Operational summary for filing authority, Basic Business License timing, renewals, Clean Hands, documents, exemptions, and common risk points.

Requirement District of Columbia (DC)
Filing Authority DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection (DLCP), Charitable Solicitation licensing category.
Filing Method Online through the DC Business Licensing Portal for a Basic Business License application.
Filing Calendar & Triggers Initial: obtain a Basic Business License (BBL) in the Charitable Solicitation category before soliciting. Choose a 2- or 4-year term and renew by the expiration date; DLCP opens online renewal up to 90 days before expiration.
Filing Due Date License expiration (2 or 4 years from issuance). Example: License issued Mar 2024 expires Mar 31 2026 (2-yr). Renewal application must be filed by expiration (no automatic extension).
Renewal Detail Renew for the selected 2- or 4-year license term. DLCP lists charitable-solicitation fees of $99 for 2 years and $198 for 4 years. Keep corporate registration, tax registration, and Clean Hands status ready for licensing.
Extensions Calendar None formal. If BBL expires, no solicitation allowed until renewed. (DLCP may allow late renewal within 30-day grace; after that, must reapply.)
Exemptions Small, religious, etc.: Exempts nonprofits with < $25,000 in contributions annually and no paid fundraiser. Also exempts member-only solicitations, workplace campaign-only participants, religious organizations fundraising for religious/educational purposes, and very small <$1,500 revenue orgs (threshold now superseded by $25k rule).
Good Standing Required for Charity Filing? Yes. The licensing path includes corporate registration when applicable, DC tax registration, and a Clean Hands check. A foreign nonprofit operating in DC should complete the applicable Corporations Division registration before the license application.
Solicitation while Curing Deficiencies? Do not assume an expired license permits continued fundraising. Confirm current license status and renewal handling with DLCP before soliciting while a deficiency is being cured.
Enforcement Posture High (Strict). DC requires a BBL to solicit; operating without one can result in enforcement by DLCP (fines) and OAG action. The District actively monitors compliance and can impose penalties or shut down events lacking proper licensing.
Recovery Method Determine whether DLCP will accept a renewal or requires a new application, then resolve corporate, tax, Clean Hands, occupancy, or document issues identified in the application path. Ask DLCP how to handle any period of unlicensed solicitation.
Required Documents Initial: DC BBL application must include Certificate of Occupancy or Home Occupation Permit for any DC address, IRS 501(c)(3) Determination Letter, Clean Hands (tax) Cert., and corporate registration proof. Renewal: Update any info changes, ensure Clean Hands compliance, pay license fee. No financial statements or 990s required for license.
Financial Audit/Review Thresholds DLCP's current Charitable Services Business requirements list does not identify an audit or financial-statement threshold for this license. Separate grant, corporate, tax, or contractual requirements may still apply.
Schedule B Reaction Rule (Donor Names) DLCP's current public requirements list does not identify Form 990 Schedule B as a standard Charitable Solicitation license attachment. Confirm any additional document request before submitting donor information.
Signature Requirements N/A (license). DC BBL applications are signed by an authorized executive (e-sign via portal). No notarization; however, the applicant must attest to truth under penalty of perjury.
High-Risk Traps / Enforcement Notes Term choice: DLCP's license-period format changed August 1, 2025; applicants select a 2- or 4-year term. Local prerequisites: corporate registration, tax registration and Clean Hands, plus a Certificate of Occupancy or Home Occupation Permit when applicable, can lengthen the filing path. Start before a fundraising launch.

FAQ

Is DC charitable solicitation registration the same as DC charity registration?

In practice, yes. Nonprofit teams often use both phrases for the District of Columbia licensing work tied to public fundraising, Basic Business License status, and renewal timing.

Should we start with the DC registration guide or this requirements summary?

Use this page when you need the tighter filing, Clean Hands, exemption, renewal, and document cues. Use the DC registration guide when the bigger question is whether licensing is required and what path to take next.

What usually causes DC filing trouble for nonprofits?

The common problems are expired Basic Business Licenses, Clean Hands clearance issues, missing corporate registration steps for foreign nonprofits, and assuming that online fundraising can continue while a license gap is unresolved.

What if the real DC issue is renewal or delinquency instead of a new filing?

Shift into the renewal or delinquent path quickly so the organization fixes the actual DC blocker instead of treating every licensing issue like a first-time registration.
