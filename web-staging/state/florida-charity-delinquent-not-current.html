Florida expired charitable solicitation registration recovery guide

Florida charity registration recovery

Florida registration expires when the required annual renewal or extended financial statement is not filed. Recovery is therefore an approval-controlled renewal process: diagnose the FDACS record, rebuild the renewal and financial package, calculate the contribution-based fee and $25 monthly late charge, and wait for approval before treating solicitation authority as restored.

Florida at a glance

  • Regulator: Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS).
  • Expiration rule: A required record expires when the renewal or extended financial statement is not timely filed.
  • Late fee: $25 for each month or part of a month after the due date.
  • Completion: A required organization may not solicit before FDACS approves the statement.
Scope note: This page covers FDACS charitable organization or sponsor registration under the Solicitation of Contributions Act. Corporate filings, federal returns, sales-tax matters, professional fundraisers, commercial co-ventures, local permits, and other states remain separate. Confirm the FDACS record and the reason it expired

Search Check-A-Charity by the legal name and registration number, then save the status, expiration, reported financial period, public names, and date checked. Compare that record with the last approval, renewal notice, account email, and any deficiency letter. Florida law says registration expires when a required organization fails to file its renewal statement or fails to provide the financial statement within an allowed extension period.

Do not assume every missing or expired record has the same cause. The organization may have missed the renewal, relied on an incomplete financial extension, changed names, exceeded the small-charity threshold, used a professional fundraiser, or stopped soliciting. Also test any claimed statutory exemption against current facts. A corporation record or IRS determination does not replace FDACS approval.

Control solicitation until the statement is approved

Section 496.405 provides that a required charitable organization or sponsor may not solicit contributions, have another person solicit on its behalf, or participate in a charitable or sponsor sales promotion before FDACS approves its initial or annual statement. That approval boundary is especially important after expiration: an online receipt or mailed packet does not by itself recreate authority.

Inventory Florida-facing donation pages, email and direct mail, events, text campaigns, peer-to-peer tools, paid media, grant solicitations, commercial co-ventures, and professional-solicitor activity. Route operational decisions through qualified counsel when the facts require it. The page explains the filing workflow; it does not decide whether a particular communication is a regulated solicitation.

Use the renewal channel tied to the existing record

FDACS says the easiest renewal path is online. A registrant that has not previously filed online can create an account. For paper renewal, the agency directs organizations to the preprinted renewal application sent about 30 days before expiration and says the forms posted for new organizations are not a substitute. A filer that did not receive the renewal can request it from FDACS.

Use the existing number and current legal name, then reconcile addresses, responsible people, related organizations, solicitors, consultants, commercial co-ventures, disciplinary history, and the prior fiscal period. Report officer or director changes within the statute’s shorter update rule when applicable. Keep one controlled dataset so the renewal, financial statement, federal return, contracts, and public disclosures agree.

Rebuild financial support and calculate both fees

The annual financial statement reports the immediately preceding fiscal year. The filing may use the applicable Form 990 information and requires the state schedules and financial support called for by the current application. Florida’s financial-statement depth changes at contribution thresholds, including review or audit requirements at higher levels. Use the current form and statute rather than last year’s workpaper.

Calculate the registration fee from the contribution bands in section 496.405 and keep it separate from the late charge. FDACS publishes a $25 late fee for each month or part of a month after the renewal and financial report were due. If an approved IRS-based financial extension existed, save the submitted Form 8868 and the FDACS extension evidence; the extension covers financial information, not a blanket right to leave the rest of the registration incomplete.

Verify approval, disclosures, and the next date

Save the complete renewal, financial materials, contracts, fee workpaper, submission confirmation, payment receipt, deficiency responses, and approval. Then search Check-A-Charity again and confirm the legal name, registration number, current status, expiration, and public financial period. Treat the approval and updated record as the completion evidence.

Review the Florida disclosure statement on solicitations, confirmations, receipts, reminders, and the applicable donation webpage. The required language must include the state toll-free number and FDACS website and must not imply endorsement. Calendar the next expiration, the document collection start, the extension decision date, and a status recheck. Florida recovery FAQ

Why does a Florida charity registration expire?

Florida law provides for expiration when a required renewal statement is not filed or the financial statement is not supplied within an allowed extension period.

Can a Florida charity solicit while a late renewal is pending?

A required organization may not solicit or have another person solicit on its behalf before FDACS approves the statement.

Can a late Florida charity use the new-organization form?

FDACS directs paper renewals to the preprinted renewal application tied to the existing record; the publicly posted new-organization form is not the paper renewal substitute.

What is the Florida late renewal fee?

FDACS publishes $25 for each month or part of a month after the annual renewal and financial report due date.

Does a financial extension extend the whole Florida registration?

No. The published route concerns the financial statement. Preserve the FDACS extension evidence and complete the rest of the registration requirements.

When is Florida charity recovery complete?

Use FDACS approval and the updated Check-A-Charity record showing the correct organization, status, expiration, and financial period.
