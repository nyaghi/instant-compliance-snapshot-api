Maine expired charity license recovery guide

Maine expired charity license recovery

Maine uses two different paths after a charitable-organization license expires on November 30. A late renewal can be filed within 90 days with the renewal and late fees; after that window, the state says the organization must satisfy the requirements governing a new applicant and reapply.

Maine at a glance

  • Regulator: Maine Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation, Charitable Solicitations Act.
  • Ordinary expiration: November 30 each year.
  • Late-renewal window: Up to 90 days after expiration, with the published late fee.
  • After 90 days: The state says the organization must meet new-applicant requirements and reapply.
Scope note: This page covers the Maine Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation charitable-organization license. Maine corporate records, federal tax exemption, professional-solicitor licensing, gaming, local obligations, and another state’s registration are separate. Confirm the license record and count from November 30

Begin with Maine’s official license-verification service. Save the exact legal name, license number, license type, displayed status, expiration date, disciplinary information if any, and lookup date. Maine’s charitable-organization license ordinarily expires November 30. Count the elapsed days from the actual expiration and save any renewal reminder, prior application, correspondence, or pending transaction.

Separate the charity license from Maine corporate standing and the organization’s federal tax record. An active corporation or accepted Form 990 does not renew the charitable solicitations license. If the entity believes it is exempt, review the current statutory exemption and obtain an agency determination where appropriate rather than assuming that a prior exemption or low contribution year continues indefinitely.

Use the late-renewal path within 90 days

Maine law permits a charitable-organization license to be renewed up to 90 days after expiration by paying the late fee in addition to the renewal fee. The agency page identifies the late period as December 1 through March 1, lists a $20 annual renewal fee, and assesses a $50 late fee after November 30. Use the live online renewal record to confirm the exact deadline and amount for the organization.

The late-renewal packet includes the completed renewal application and supporting documents plus the Annual Fundraising Activity Report. Preserve the submitted application, activity report, $20 renewal fee, $50 late fee, confirmation, and agency messages. Do not describe payment alone as restored licensure, and do not promise that a late renewal retroactively authorizes activity during an expired period.

Reapply when the license has been expired more than 90 days

Maine’s licensing page states that an application filed more than 90 days after expiration is subject to all requirements governing new applicants and that the organization must reapply. The state uses a Maine-specific application and does not accept the Unified Registration Statement. The current application fee is $20, subject to the live online application and agency instructions.

Maine law generally requires a nonexempt charitable organization to apply at least 30 days before soliciting, accepting, or obtaining contributions in the state and prohibits covered activity before initial licensure. Document donation pages, appeals, events, grant requests, and professional-solicitor activity during the gap and obtain appropriate advice about interim decisions. A reapplication is not a backdated renewal.

Reconcile the Annual Fundraising Activity Report

The Maine report covers the preceding fiscal year and identifies the charity, license number, fiscal period, campaign dates, contributions raised by campaign and for the year, and whether the organization contracted with a professional solicitor. For each professional solicitor, the form captures its Maine license number, campaign dates, contributions raised, and the amount retained. The organization certifies that the report is true and accurate.

Reconcile the charity’s report to campaign ledgers, deposits, contracts, invoices, and each solicitor’s own report. Maine law says a missing activity report or a disagreement between the organization’s and professional solicitor’s reports can result in disciplinary action. The organization may not contract with an unlicensed professional solicitor. Keep fundraising campaign books and records for three years after each campaign ends.

Verify issuance and rebuild the forward calendar

After renewal or reapplication, save the complete submission, fee evidence, activity report, correspondence, and issued license. Recheck the official license-verification record for the exact organization, new status, and expiration date. A portal confirmation or credit-card receipt is not a substitute for the issued license and updated state record.

Finish the recovery file with a November 30 calendar entry, a preparation start date before the expected renewal reminder, named renewal and fundraising-data owners, and a three-year record-retention control. Maine recovery FAQ

When does a Maine charitable-organization license expire?

The state says the license ordinarily expires on November 30 each year.

Can a Maine charity renew after November 30?

Yes. Maine permits late renewal for up to 90 days after expiration with the renewal fee and published $50 late fee.

What happens after the Maine license has been expired more than 90 days?

The agency says the organization must satisfy new-applicant requirements and reapply using the Maine-specific application.

Does Maine accept the Unified Registration Statement?

No. Maine’s licensing page says charitable organizations must use the Maine-specific application.

What belongs in the Maine annual fundraising activity report?

It reports the preceding fiscal year, campaign dates, contributions raised, and professional-solicitor details including license numbers and amounts retained.

How should a charity prove Maine license recovery?

Save the complete submission and fees, obtain the issued license, and verify the updated official license record and new November 30 expiration date.
