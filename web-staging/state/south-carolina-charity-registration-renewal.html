South Carolina annual charity filing guide

South Carolina charity registration renewal

South Carolina coordinates an annual registration statement with a separate annual financial report. Both generally use the four-and-one-half-month fiscal-year deadline, the registration carries a $50 fee, and a missing financial report can block renewal.

South Carolina at a glance

  • Regulator: South Carolina Secretary of State, Public Charities Division.
  • General deadline: Within four and one-half months after fiscal year end.
  • Filing channel: South Carolina Charities Online or current paper forms.
  • Extension: Request the financial-report extension through the portal before the due date.
Scope note: This guide separates the state charity filing from federal tax and entity-maintenance filings. It is general information, not legal advice. Confirm the live regulator record and obtain professional advice for the organization’s facts.

Calendar the registration and financial report as linked filings

The South Carolina deadline chart lists both the Registration Statement for a Charitable Organization and the Annual Financial Report as due annually within four and one-half months after fiscal year end. For a December 31 year end, use May 15. The registration form also warns that a paper renewal cannot be accepted more than six weeks before the current expiration.

Treat the two filings as linked but distinct. The registration updates the organization and solicitation record and carries the annual fee. The financial report covers the completed fiscal year. The state says an organization is ineligible to renew until delinquent financial statements are filed.

Prepare the $50 registration statement and officer record

The annual registration fee is $50. The form calls for the legal name, EIN, purpose, offices, registered agent, fundraising methods, other jurisdictions, prior regulatory actions, and relationships among board members and fundraising vendors. The chief executive and chief financial officers certify the filing.

Reconcile professional fundraiser, fundraising counsel, commercial co-venturer, raffle, and campaign records before renewal. The online instructions say outstanding raffle reports and joint financial reports can block completion of the related renewal workflow.

Choose an accepted annual financial report

South Carolina accepts Form 990, 990-EZ, or 990-PF, or the Secretary of State’s Annual Financial Report Form. The online guidance expressly says it does not accept Form 990-N as the state financial report; a postcard filer should complete the state form instead.

  • Use the same fiscal period on the registration and financial report.
  • Reconcile direct public support, events, commercial co-ventures, professional fundraising, program services, fundraising expense, management expense, assets, and liabilities.
  • Complete the officer certifications required by the filing method.
  • Upload the final accepted report rather than an unsigned draft or an IRS extension without the state request.

Use the Financial Reports tab for the state extension

The online instructions direct renewing organizations to the Financial Reports tab when the financial report will not be ready by the deadline. State law allows a written request or a copy of the IRS extension request, but the safe operational control is to complete the South Carolina portal step by the original due date and save the state response.

An extension of the financial report does not eliminate the registration work. Keep the $50 registration, organization record, and outstanding ancillary reports moving while the final return or state financial report is completed.

Verify accepted status and manage a delinquency quickly

Online submissions enter a review queue and display Pending until the office accepts them. The state sends the contact person a confirmation letter when a registration is accepted. Save that letter, the payment receipt, the filed financial report, and the dashboard status.

South Carolina law permits an administrative fine of $10 per day for a delinquent annual financial report, up to $2,000 for each violation, and can bar further solicitation or renewal. If a filing is late, first clear every outstanding financial report, registration, raffle, or campaign item, then confirm the public Charities Search reflects the result.

South Carolina renewal FAQ

When is a South Carolina charity renewal due?

The state deadline chart lists the annual registration and financial report within four and one-half months after fiscal year end.

What is the South Carolina charity registration fee?

The annual registration statement carries a $50 fee.

Can South Carolina accept Form 990-N as the financial report?

No. The online instructions say to use Form 990, 990-EZ, 990-PF, or the Secretary of State’s Annual Financial Report Form.

How does a charity request a South Carolina financial-report extension?

A renewing charity uses the Financial Reports tab in the online account before the report due date and saves the state response.

What happens if the annual financial report is late?

The organization can become ineligible to renew and may face a $10-per-day administrative fine up to $2,000 for each violation.
