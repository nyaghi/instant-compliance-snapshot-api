Use the right source for the question

A registration decision, a filing packet, and an annual renewal are different research tasks. Start with the source that owns the question, record the date reviewed, and keep the link beside the decision. Search snippets and third-party charts are useful for orientation, but the regulator's current page, instructions, portal, or statute should support the final operational step.

Board-ready fundraising compliance checklist

Before approving a campaign

  • Name the campaign channels, audiences, states, fundraiser relationships, and launch date.
  • Review public registration status and open notices in the states already on the footprint.
  • Identify new-state registration or exemption decisions and the official sources supporting them.
  • Confirm required solicitation disclosures and the owner responsible for creative review.

After approving a campaign

  • Save filed forms, attachments, payment receipts, confirmation numbers, and acceptance notices.
  • Place renewal dates and document-start dates in one calendar with a named owner and backup.
  • Monitor material changes in donor geography, paid promotion, platform use, and fundraising contracts.
  • Escalate rejected, late, delinquent, or not-current results into a documented recovery plan.

This checklist is an operational starting point. It does not decide whether a particular organization is required to register and is not legal or tax advice.
