Expansion states

Status key

Current
Registration appears active/current based on the state source or CharityClarity coverage data.
Upcoming Filing
Registration appears current, with a filing or renewal due soon.
Pending / Filing Pending
The state source shows a pending registration, filing, or renewal.
Registered
The state source confirms registration, but CharityClarity could not determine a more specific current or filing-cycle status.
Delinquent
The state source indicates the registration or required filing is late, expired, overdue, or not current.
Failed to Renew
The state source indicates the organization did not renew or did not complete a required renewal.
Suspended
The state source indicates the organization is suspended or not authorized to solicit.
Revoked
The state source indicates the registration or exemption has been revoked.
Closed / Withdrawn / Canceled
The state source indicates the registration or entity record is closed, withdrawn, inactive, canceled, or terminated.
Exempt
The state source indicates the organization is exempt from registration.
Not Registered
The state search completed and no qualifying registration record was found.
No Confirmed Match
Organization found in CharityClarity coverage list; CharityClarity searched the state source and did not find a safe matching nonprofit record.
Outside Coverage
The organization is not in the current CharityClarity coverage list for that state.
Unable to Confirm
CharityClarity could not complete or trust the source check because the source was unavailable, unreachable, blocked, ambiguous, or did not provide enough evidence.

Coverage list means the state-specific organizations CharityClarity has included in this staging source set. For Utah, the current coverage list is focused on IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) organizations with reported income above $1 million that are regular Form 990/990-EZ filers; smaller organizations, churches, religious organizations not required to file Form 990, state-only entities, and other non-regular filers may not be included.

This lab is intentionally separate from the 30-state CharityClarity product. Some expansion sources require human verification or login; CharityClarity reports those limits conservatively instead of inferring a status.
